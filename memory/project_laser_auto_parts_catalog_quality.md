**Image sourcing — fast method (developed 2026-09-11, dramatically faster than an earlier slow per-product browser-navigation approach):**
- Source: autodoc.parts, browsed by exact OEM number at `https://www.autodoc.parts/car-parts/oem/<compact-uppercase-sku>` (strip spaces/dashes, strip anything after `/`, strip a leading store-specific "C " prefix, uppercase then lowercase for the URL).
